Privacy & Security

The short version

This site does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no forms.

Stored on your device

The site remembers your display choices in your browser's local storage, under one key, tg-prefs. It never leaves your device:

  • mode: light or dark
  • char: the character palette you picked
  • sfx / music: whether sound effects and music are on (both start off)
  • simple: whether animations are turned off
  • expLayout: whether the Experience section shows as a list or a map

To erase it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Third parties

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address for security and abuse prevention, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Nothing else loads from elsewhere. Fonts, images, sounds and scripts are all served from this site, so opening a page contacts GitHub Pages and nobody else.
  • Links out. LinkedIn, GitHub, Credly, YouTube, Google Drive and UiPath links take you to those sites, which have their own policies.

How the site protects you

  • A Content Security Policy only allows scripts from this site, blocks inline scripts, plugins and form submissions, and only allows fonts from this site.
  • Every external link opens with rel="noopener", so other sites can't control this tab.
  • The referrer policy only shares the site's address, never the full page path, with other sites.

Report a security issue

Found a vulnerability? Email garciatheodoreiii715@gmail.com. Contact details are also published at /.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116). Please don't test against third-party services linked from this site.

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected, there's nothing to access, correct or delete. If you emailed me, I keep only what's needed to reply, and you can ask me to delete it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
